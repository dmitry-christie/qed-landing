Questions

The stuff people ask.

How far ahead should we book?
For November and December, as early as you can: December Thursdays and Fridays go first, and so do rooms for big groups. The rest of the year, two weeks is usually enough. Tighter than that? Ask anyway.
How much does it cost?
From €7 + VAT per person, with a minimum of €250 + VAT per event. The final price depends on headcount, city and whether we provide the venue, and you get it as one fixed quote with no surprises.
What's included?
A live host, our AV kit (sound, a screen or projector and live scoring), custom rounds about your company and prizes for the winning team, plus an operator for larger groups. Extras on request: custom trophies and a photographer.
Can it be a lunch or an afternoon?
Yes. Plenty of teams prefer a company lunch or an afternoon plan. Same quiz, same host, just earlier.
We've booked the restaurant. Do you come there?
Yes, that's the usual setup. We play before or after the meal, sort the space out with the restaurant and bring our own kit if needed.
How does the date hold work?
After the call we hold the date for up to 7 days at no cost (72 h on December Fridays and Saturday 12th, which go fast). To confirm, just accept the quote.
How many people can play?
Anywhere from 10 to 300+. Smaller teams play as individuals or pairs; bigger groups split into tables. Big groups are no trouble at all.
How long does it run?
About 90 minutes for the full night, though we flex it around your schedule. A tight after-workshop slot or a long evening both work.
Do you travel to us?
Both formats are hosted in person across Valencia, Madrid, Barcelona, Murcia and Santiago de Compostela. Somewhere else? Pick "Elsewhere" in the form and tell us where.
Our office, or a venue?
Either. We'll run it in your space with our AV, or recommend and coordinate a venue that fits your group and budget.
English or Spanish?
Both, and we mix them for international teams. Tell us your room and we'll pitch it right.
