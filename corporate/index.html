What it is

Not your usual pub quiz.

A game show for your team, with a host who runs the room. 90 minutes that fly by.

In teams

Every table is a team. You play by talking, arguing and scribbling answers.

Not exam questions

Music, pictures, maps, emojis and flags, plus a few surprises along the way.

A live leaderboard

The scores go up on screen after every round. The rivalry starts in round two.

One team wins

And at least one answer gets argued about until dessert.

Why companies book it

Everyone plays. Really.

No need to be sporty

No running, no climbing, no trust falls. You play sitting down, drink in hand.

Or outgoing, or a singer

Nobody has to get up on stage. The quiet ones play from the table (and often get it right).

Everyone knows something

Finance nails the music round and the intern knows every flag. That's why the teams mix so well.

Questions

The stuff people ask.

How far ahead should we book?
For December, as early as you can: Fridays and Saturday 12th fill first. After the call we hold your date for up to 7 days, no commitment (72 hours on those dates). The rest of the year, two weeks is usually enough.
How much is it, and what's included?
From €150 + VAT per event (€181.50 incl. VAT). It goes by group size (€200, €250 or €300 + VAT up to 75 people; more than 75, we quote it) and it's €50 less at one of our venues. The host, the quiz, live scoring, sound and screen are included. Rounds about your company, prizes, trophies, live rounds and a photographer are extras we quote on the call.
We've booked a restaurant (or it's at the office). Do you come there?
Yes, that's the usual setup. We play before or after the meal and sort it out with the place. We bring our own sound and screen; if there's already a screen or projector and sound, we use theirs, which keeps it simple. All we need is a power socket and a little time to set up.
Do you organise the offsite or the dinner too?
No, our part is the quiz. The dinner, catering, travel and the rest of the offsite are yours to organise (or your event organiser's). No venue yet? We'll recommend one, ideally one of our venues: it's €50 cheaper because they're already set up for the quiz.
How long is it, and when?
About 90 minutes. At lunchtime, in the afternoon or in the evening, right before or after the meal: we fit it to your schedule.
How many people can play?
From 10 to 300+, in teams at each table. Nobody needs to know everything: there are picture, music and map rounds, and the host keeps it moving so nobody's left out.
English or Spanish?
Either, or bilingual for international teams. We have hosts in both languages.
Which cities do you cover?
Valencia, Madrid, Barcelona, Murcia and Santiago de Compostela. Somewhere else? Pick "Elsewhere" in the form and we'll look into it. Outside Valencia we work with local hosts and venues, and some services (such as live rounds, the photographer or our own sound kit) depend on availability. We'll confirm it on the call.
Do you invoice? How do we pay?
Yes, a VAT invoice made out to your company. You pay by bank transfer or by card (we send you a payment link), before the event and at the latest the day before. For Friday 11 and Saturday 12 December, 30% is paid when you confirm.
What if the date or the headcount changes?
You can change the headcount until 3 working days before, and the price follows the tier. One date change is free if you tell us at least 7 days ahead, subject to availability. Cancelling is free up to 7 days before; after that, the 11-12 December deposit isn't refunded, but you can use it for another date within 3 months.
Can we see it before we book?
Yes. Come to one of our public quiz nights with a couple of colleagues and see it live. The dates are on quizeatdrink.com.

We know trivia. We run open quiz nights every week and write our own questions: what hooks a bar full of strangers will hook your team too.
