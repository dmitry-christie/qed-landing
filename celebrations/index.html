Questions

What everyone wants to know.

How personalised does it get?
As much as you like. The personalised round is an extra: we build a whole round (or more) from the details and photos you send about the guest of honour or the couple, then balance it with general questions so everyone can join in.
How many guests can play?
From an intimate 2-table dinner up to a 200-guest party. Small groups play in pairs, big ones split into teams with a live leaderboard.
Do you travel to us?
We run it in person in Valencia, Madrid, Barcelona, Murcia and Santiago de Compostela, at a venue or at your place. We bring the host, the sound and the screen (or use the room's own, if it has them); all we need is a socket and a little time to set up. Outside Valencia we work with local hosts and venues, and some services (like live rounds, the photographer or our own sound kit) depend on availability. We'll confirm it on the call.
Is it family-friendly?
It can be. Tell us the crowd and we'll pitch it accordingly, all-ages for a family birthday, or a little spicier for a stag-adjacent night.
English or Spanish?
Both, and we mix them for international guest lists. Perfect for a wedding with two sides who don't share a first language.
How far ahead should we book?
Two weeks is usually enough (and gives us time to write the personal round, if you add it). Closer to the date? Ask anyway, we'll always try to make it work.
