Questions

What people ask before they start.

How much do I need to invest to start?
€99 + VAT onboarding, no monthly fee, and nothing to pay until you sign. Beyond that you need a laptop that runs Chrome and printed answer sheets; the venue usually has the screen and sound. Team Mac? No drama: install Chrome, it's free and it's where we test everything. Other browsers may work, but we wouldn't bet the trophy on it. No fit-out, no expensive kit. Ads over the first months come on top, and we size them with you.
What does the €99 + VAT onboarding include?
  • Initial training to learn how to run and host the quiz nights.
  • Setting up your franchise profile on our platform.
  • Access to the management dashboard.
  • Setting up your events and bookings page.
  • Launching your local social media accounts.
  • The digital brand materials you need for launch.
  • Your first full set of questions.
  • Online support through the early phase.
How much can I earn?
It depends on how many players fill the room, the ticket price (you set it) and how many nights a week you run. Ticket money goes into your own Stripe account, and we invoice you for our share monthly, after the fact. On the call we run the numbers for your city.
Do I need my own venue?
No. You can run it in bars you don't own, and the deal with each one is yours to strike: a fee, a cut of the bar, or simply filling a quiet night for them. Their licence does need to cover ticketed events. No venue yet? Say so in the form and we'll help you find one.
Do I need experience? Do I have to host it myself?
Neither. Onboarding includes an online workshop, scripts and training for whoever will host: you or someone on your team. What counts is consistency, a night every week, run with energy. And through your first nights, we stay close.
How long is the contract? What if it doesn't work out?
We walk you through the length, the renewal and how to leave on the first call. Then you get it all in writing in the pre-contract document, 20 business days before signing, so you can go through it calmly (or with your adviser) with nothing signed or paid.
Do I get exclusivity in my area?
Yes. Each venue you run gets a protected 2.5 km radius on its night of the week: while it stays active, we won't sign anyone else for a Quiz Eat Drink night that day inside it. Spanish-language quizzes (a separate product) and the private events we sell ourselves sit outside it: if one lands near you, we tell you and, if it fits, offer it to you to host, paid.
Do I have to spend on ads?
It's strongly recommended over the first months, while the room fills. You set the budget and we run the campaigns: you see the spend and the results at all times, and we pause them when a night sells out. If you already have an audience or channels that work, we scale it down or drop it.
Do I need to be self-employed (autónomo) or have a company?
Not necessarily you: a company can sign (yours or a partner's), or the venue itself. Whoever signs takes the ticket money into their own Stripe account and invoices it, so they need to be able to invoice. If someone has to register, there's time in the 20 business days before signing.
Can I run more than one venue, or more than one city?
Yes. You start with one venue and, once it runs steadily, you add more, in your city or another. Each new venue gets its own protected radius. On the call we'll tell you what the first one needs to hit before you add the next.
Can I run it in Spanish too?
In principle, yes. The Spanish-language format is Tardeo de Trivia, a separate company, so it would be two contracts, one with each. If you're interested, mention it in the form.
