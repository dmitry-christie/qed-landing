The model

Low to start. Built to compound.

A small setup fee, the platform itself at no extra charge, and a share of what the nights bring in. We recommend spending on ads over the first months, while the room fills. If you already have an audience of your own, or your own channels, you may not need to. No surprises buried in the contract.

You have a venue

A bar, a restaurant, a club. You fill your quietest weeknight with a room that eats and drinks while it plays, and the bar stays yours.

You run events

No venue of your own. You build the circuit: you find the rooms, agree the deal with each one, and run the night. Add venues, add cities, and it stacks.

Either way you get the same platform, the same brand and the same content. What changes is where the room comes from.

Questions

The stuff franchisees ask.

Do I get exclusive rights to my area?

Yes. Each partner gets a protected 2.5km radius around each venue they run, for the same day of the week: while that venue is active, we won't sign another partner running a Quiz Eat Drink night that day inside that radius. Run several rooms and each one carries its own radius.

Tardeo de Trivia, our Spanish-language quiz brand, is a different product with a different audience and is run separately.

Two things sit outside the radius, so you know up front. Private and corporate events that we sell and host ourselves: we can run those anywhere, your area included, and we keep that revenue. When one lands near you we tell you, and if it fits we offer you the hosting. And each language is a separate product, so the two brands can run the same night in the same street.

Can I run more than one city?
Yes, it's possible. We'll go through the details on the first call.
Do I need my own venue?
No. You can run in rooms you don't own. The deal with each venue is yours to strike: a fee, a cut of the bar, or simply the covers you fill on a quiet night. Bring what you have in mind to the call and we'll work through it with you.
Do I need experience hosting trivia nights?
No. Full onboarding and training are included. You bring the rooms and the personality, we bring the platform, content and brand.
What happens to the ad spend over time?
We recommend it over the first months, while you build an audience, and it scales down with turnout after that. If you already have an audience of your own, or channels that work, you may not need it. Let's talk: our only goal is making the event a success, and we're on the same team.
